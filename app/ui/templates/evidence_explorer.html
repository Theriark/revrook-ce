The rookery wall of {{ view.client_name }}

Each pillar is an evidence export, as tall as the number of controls it feeds; each tile is a control, grouped by domain around the wall; a beam joins an export to every control that reads it. Drag to turn the wall, or use the buttons. A pillar that is stale is low, an export never imported is only a footprint, and a tile that is not protected is cut through.

The scene needs script and WebGL 2 in the browser. The tables below show the same facts.

Point at a pillar or a tile, or press Show in scene on a row below, to read it here.

  • ✓ Protected flat tile
  • ▲ Needs attention tile with a ring
  • ✗ Not protected tile cut through
  • ? Not checked outlined tile
  • – Not applicable small disc
  • ✓ Current export full pillar
  • ▲ Stale or expired export low pillar
  • ? Not imported footprint only

What this view is

A scene drawn in the browser from the same checks as the checklist of {{ view.client_name }} at {{ view.as_of[:16].replace("T", " ") }} UTC. It stores nothing, loads nothing from outside this installation, and is not part of any report. The tables are the record; the scene is a way of looking at them, like the rookery skyline of Home and the domain keep of a client overview.
