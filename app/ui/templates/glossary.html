Glossary

The words revrook uses, each explained once, in the order you meet them.

Pack date
The date the evidence was read. Everything in a report is true as of that date.
Evidence
An export from a tool, a document or a statement, with the time it was captured. A status with no dated evidence is shown as Not checked.
Automatic export, manual artefact, attestation
How much a piece of evidence proves. An automatic export is read from a tool's own file. A manual artefact is a document a named person reviewed on a date. An attestation is a statement with no document behind it, and counts for the least.
Freshness
Evidence counts in full up to its maximum age, counts less after that, and counts for nothing at twice that age.
Coverage
How much of the score rests on evidence that is present and fresh. Coverage is shown beside the score so that better evidence is not read as better security.
Best case
The score if every control that was not checked, or whose evidence is old, turned out to pass.
Lower bound
A score that is shown as the least the client could have, because too little was checked to give a real score.
Gate control
A control so important that, if it is not protected, it holds the whole score down.
Protected, Needs attention, Not protected, Not checked
The four statuses of a control. Each has an icon and words, so it can be told apart without colour. Not checked means nothing was verified.
Exception
A control that is knowingly not met, with an owner, a reason and a date to look at it again. It never changes a status.
QBR
Quarterly business review: the report you give a client each quarter.
MFA
Multi-factor authentication: signing in with more than a password.
SPF, DKIM, DMARC
Three public settings of a mail domain that help receivers tell real mail from forged mail. SPF lists the servers allowed to send, DKIM signs the mail and DMARC says what to do when a check fails.
Snapshot
A stored copy of the state of a client on one day. A report is made from a snapshot, so it can be made again exactly.

What a report is

This product summarises settings observed on a date. It is not a certification, audit or attestation.
