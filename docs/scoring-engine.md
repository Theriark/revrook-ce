# The scoring engine and snapshots (phase 6)

`app/scoring` turns the results of the check engine into the score of one client at
one date. It is pure (no clock, database or network) and exact: every value is a
`fractions.Fraction`, there is no float in the engine and a test fails if one
appears. The model is section 3.5 of rnd-report-3. What the check engine hands over,
and the sensitivity result for the weights, are in [scoring.md](scoring.md).

| Part | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Control score | `s = r x q x f`. r is 1 for a pass, a fixed one half for a partial, 0 for a fail, unknown or not-applicable control. q is 1, 0.9 or 0.6 (0 without evidence). |
| Freshness `f` | 1 up to the maximum age M, then `1 - (A - M) / M`, 0 from 2M on. An age exactly M gives 1, 1.5 M gives one half, 2 M gives 0. A time after the pack date counts as age 0. Evidence without a time, or a control without a limit, is fresh. |
| Domain `D` | `100 x sum(weight x s) / Wa`, Wa the weight of the controls that are not not-applicable. Unknown and stale controls stay in Wa. |
| Overall `O` | the domain-weighted mean of D over the domains that are not dropped. A domain whose controls are all not-applicable is dropped and its weight leaves the denominator. |
| Gate caps | A gate control that fails, is unknown, or has expired evidence (f = 0) caps the score at 49. A gate control that is partial, or stale with f above 0, caps it at 69. `S = min(O, cap)`; the lowest cap wins, the first gate on a tie, and the cap and its reason are kept beside the raw score even when they do not bind. A not-applicable gate (with its second reviewer) does not cap. |
| Coverage | The weight of controls with fresh pass, partial or fail evidence over the weight of controls that are not not-applicable. A stale control is not covered. |
| Best case `U` | The score with every unknown and stale control set to a pass at q 1 and f 1, capped by the gate rule on that state. A fresh fail or partial is not improved, so a failed gate is not missing evidence. |
| Bands | 85 and over Strong, 70 Good, 50 Fair, 30 Weak, below 30 Critical. The band follows the exact score, not its rounded display (84.96 is shown as 85.0 and is Good). |
| Thin evidence | Coverage below 0.60 gives the band Insufficient evidence and no best case: the number is a lower bound only. |
| Not-applicable weight | Above 20 percent of the total weight raises `na_flagged`. |

Shown as `Score 45.7 (best case 69.0), band Weak, evidence coverage 71 percent`
(`app.scoring.display.headline`); a capped score adds `capped from 86.6 by
PC-ACC-02`. Scores are shown to one decimal, halves rounded away from zero.

## Entry points

`score_evidence(library, evidence)` runs the check engine and scores its results;
`score_results` scores results already made; `score_inputs` scores plain
`ControlInput` values (status, quality, evidence time, maximum age, weight, domain,
gate), which is what a stored snapshot holds. A naive `as_of` (or evidence time) is
refused with a `ValueError` before anything is evaluated, and so is a control with an
unknown domain, a duplicate, or no result: a control is never left out silently.

## Snapshots

`app/scoring/snapshot.py` stores everything a score was made from: ruleset id and
version, importer version, pack date, the thresholds of the model, the domain weights,
the evidence items (id and SHA-256) and each control's status, quality, evidence time,
maximum age, weight, domain and gate, then the per-control and overall results. The
text is canonical JSON (sorted keys, no spaces, ASCII, one line, a final newline,
fractions as `n/d`, times in UTC, no float) whose last key is the SHA-256 of all the
others. `regenerate` reads only the stored inputs and scores them again, so a later
change to a check rule or to the control file cannot alter a pack already made;
`verify_snapshot` fails unless the regenerated bytes are the stored ones, and an edited
pack is refused by its digest.

`tests/scoring_vectors/golden-snapshot-client-b-pc-rules-0.1.json` is such an old
snapshot. It is written once per ruleset version by `tools/make_scoring_golden.py`
and never rewritten while the version stands; if scoring changes, the ruleset version
changes and the old file stays.

Trends compare only packs of the same ruleset and the same major version
(`ruleset_major`). Version 0 promises no stability, so for 0.x the minor number counts
too: PC-RULES 0.1 and 0.2 do not compare. Score and coverage change are returned
separately so that better evidence is not mistaken for better security.

## The vectors and the properties

`tests/scoring_vectors/clients.json` holds clients A, B and C of phase-plan-3 and
`tests/test_scoring_vectors.py` checks them within 0.05 together with the seven edge
vectors. The hand vectors were reproduced without bending the engine: A scores 89.5
(best case 90.5, coverage 97 of 98), B 45.7 (best case 69.0, coverage 70 of 99) and C
86.6 capped to 49.0 by PC-ACC-02. Two readings had to be made where the plan is silent
and are recorded in the file: the statuses of the LOG and GOV controls of client B (the
plan gives only their earned weight, 3.0 and 3.4, and the best-case domain scores), and
that a stale control counts as missing evidence in the best case whatever its status.
These vectors are hand-computed. The independent recomputation (the Test Engineer's)
is `tests/independent/recompute_vectors.py`: it does not import the app, types
the scoring model again from the plan with its own control table and checks that table
against `data/controls.yaml` (the control file wins). Run it with
`python3 -m unittest tests/independent/recompute_vectors.py`.

`tests/test_scoring_properties.py` checks the six properties of rnd-report-3 section
3.5 over 600 random cases each (adding or refreshing evidence never lowers a score;
unknown scores 0 and removing evidence never raises S; stale decays to 0 by 2M;
manual-only controls reach at most 0.9 of their weight; gates cap the headline; S is a
lower bound and U an upper bound of every completion, and deleting a random 20 percent
of the evidence never raises S). hypothesis is not in the development lock, so the
cases are drawn with `random.Random` and a fixed seed.

## Limits

- The reason of a not-applicable decision is not escaped here; the reports of phase 8
  escape every text they print (`docs/reports.md`, tested with a hostile reason).
- Freshness is measured from the evidence time to the pack date in exact time, so a
  control can be stale for less than a day (`age_days` of the check engine is whole
  days).
- The weights, thresholds and bands are our own uncalibrated design choices.
